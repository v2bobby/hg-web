THE STATUS QUO

The recruitment friction crisis
no one wants to admit.

The Resume Paradox

Generative AI and professional CV services have made resumes unreliable proxies for skill. Recruiters spend an average of 6 seconds scanning each one. Good talent gets buried. Risky hires slip through.

The Ghosting Epidemic

Over 75% of job seekers never hear back after applying. This silence creates negative brand sentiment, emotional fatigue, and reduces the quality of future applicant pools.

Verification Failure

The rise of ghost companies and fake job listings has destroyed trust in digital job boards. Candidates are applying to opportunities that don't exist. Employers can't be sure who they're talking to.

THE SHIFT

We don't improve the old system.
We replace it with infrastructure.

HiredeyGo is a two-sided trust layer that turns every application into a verified performance and guarantees every candidate a response.

THE SKILL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

Trust enforced through
structured assessments & feedback loops.

Employer Protocol

The Quality Gate

Verification Layer
No employer can post without TIN/KYC/Business Registration verification. Candidates only apply to legitimate opportunities.
Custom Challenge Builder
Recruiters create role-specific "Challenge Sets" — a precise mix of questions that reflect actual day-to-day requirements.

Candidate Protocol

The Transparency Engine

Independent Assessment
Timed, proctored tests via browser-lock or activity monitoring. Every submission is a real performance.
Living Profile + Progress
Candidates see exactly where they stand in every pipeline in real time. No more black holes.

The Ranking Engine
