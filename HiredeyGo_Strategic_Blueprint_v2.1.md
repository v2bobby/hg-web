### Executive Summary

HiredeyGo is the trust infrastructure for modern recruitment. It solves the two most persistent failures in hiring today:

- **Manual Screening Fatigue** for employers  
- **Communication Ghosting** for candidates

By placing a mandatory, job-specific assessment at the exact moment of application, HiredeyGo transforms the “CV black hole” into a real-time, merit-ranked pipeline. The platform prioritizes verified skill over formatted resumes and enforces transparency at every stage.

We are not building another job board. We are building the layer that makes merit visible and silence impossible.

---

### 1. The Recruitment Friction Crisis

#### 1.1 The Resume Paradox
In an era of generative AI and professional CV writing services, resumes have become unreliable signals of actual capability. Recruiters spend an average of six seconds scanning each CV. The result: missed talent, biased decisions, and high-risk hires.

#### 1.2 The Candidate Experience Gap
More than 75% of job seekers never receive any response after applying. This systemic silence damages employer brands, creates emotional fatigue, and ultimately degrades the quality of future applicant pools.

#### 1.3 Verification Failure
The proliferation of ghost companies and fabricated job listings has eroded trust in digital recruitment platforms. Candidates waste time on non-existent opportunities. Employers cannot be certain they are engaging with real talent.

HiredeyGo addresses all three failures through a single, elegant mechanism: **mandatory verified assessment at the point of application**, combined with enforced status transparency.

---

### 2. The Skill-First Architecture

HiredeyGo operates as a two-sided marketplace where trust is structurally enforced through assessments and automated feedback loops.

#### 2.1 Employer Protocol — The Quality Gate

**Verification Layer**  
No employer may post a job without completing a rigorous verification process (TIN / KYC / Business Registration). This ensures every opportunity on the platform is legitimate.

**Custom Assessment Builder**  
Instead of generic keyword filters, recruiters design precise “Challenge Sets” — a tailored combination of multiple-choice, logic, and technical questions that mirror the actual demands of the role.

#### 2.2 Candidate Protocol — The Transparency Engine

Candidates maintain living profiles that evolve with every interaction. The application process shifts from passive submission to active performance:

- All assessments are timed and independently proctored (browser-lock or activity monitoring).  
- Candidates receive real-time visibility into their position in every recruiter’s pipeline.

#### 2.3 The Ranking Engine — Automated Merit Triage

As soon as a candidate completes an assessment, the engine calculates a composite merit score based on three objective signals:

1. **Accuracy** — Performance on the recruiter-defined challenge  
2. **Velocity** — Time taken to complete the assessment  
3. **Consistency** — Historical performance across similar role types

Recruiters are immediately presented with a “Shortlist Ready” dashboard, sorted by verified merit rather than resume formatting.

### 3. The Anti-Ghosting Framework

HiredeyGo makes transparency non-negotiable. The interface is deliberately designed to force status updates.

| Status Stage         | Recruiter Action                          | Candidate Visibility                          |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Under Review         | Assessment submitted & scored             | “Your assessment is being ranked”             |
| Shortlisted          | Marked as “Top Talent”                    | “You are in the top 10% of applicants”        |
| Interview Scheduled  | Calendar invite sent (dashboard updated)  | “Interviewing: Follow your email”             |
| Rejected / Accepted  | Final decision toggle                     | Instant notification with clear closing date  |

Recruiters who consistently fail to update candidate statuses suffer penalties to their **Employer Trust Score**, directly impacting their visibility to high-quality talent in the future.

### 4. Monetization & Value Capture

HiredeyGo captures value from both sides of the market while keeping core application free for candidates.

#### 4.1 Employer Tiers (B2B SaaS)
- **Essential (SMB)**: Pay-per-job posting + basic ranking  
- **Growth (Mid-Market)**: Unlimited postings + custom assessment templates + bulk messaging  
- **Enterprise**: Dedicated verification support + deep HRIS integrations (Workday, BambooHR, etc.)

#### 4.2 Candidate Value-Add (Freemium)
While applying remains free, **HiredeyGo Pro** offers:
- Detailed performance analytics and cohort benchmarking  
- Priority verified status badge on the platform

#### 4.3 The Data Asset
HiredeyGo generates a proprietary, high-resolution dataset on regional skill supply and demand. This intelligence is invaluable to educational institutions, governments, and workforce development organizations.
